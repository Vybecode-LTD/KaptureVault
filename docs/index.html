KaptureVault

Keystroke, clipboard & screenshot vault for Windows

Everything you type, copy, and capture — stored locally, encrypted, always yours.

⌨
Keystroke Vault
Automatically captures everything you type, grouped into sessions and searchable.
📋
Clipboard Vault
Never lose a copied snippet again. Full clipboard history with type-aware display.
🖼
Screenshot Vault
Auto-captured screenshots linked to the app you were using, with thumbnail previews.
🔒
AES-256 Encryption
Optional password-protected encryption for all vault content stored on disk.
☁
Google Drive Sync
Optionally back up and sync your encrypted vault to Google Drive across devices.
🔍
Quick Paste
Global hotkey to instantly search your vault and paste any entry into any app.
