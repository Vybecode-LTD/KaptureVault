Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 May 2026

Overview

KaptureVault is a local-first Windows desktop application. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable optional Google Drive sync. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or require accounts.

Data collected and where it is stored

KaptureVault captures the following data locally on your machine only, stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KaptureVault\:

  • Keystrokes — text typed into applications on your device.
  • Clipboard entries — text, HTML, and image data copied to the Windows clipboard.
  • Screenshots — periodic or triggered screenshots of your desktop.

All captured content is stored in a local SQLite database. Optionally, content can be protected with AES-256-GCM encryption secured by a password you choose.

Google Drive sync (optional)

When you explicitly enable Google Drive sync in Settings, KaptureVault uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope. This means the app can only access files it created — it cannot read any other file on your Drive.

The only file uploaded is the encrypted database (vault.db) stored in a KaptureVault folder on your Drive. No other data is transmitted.

OAuth tokens that allow Drive access are stored locally using Windows DPAPI (Data Protection API), which encrypts them to your Windows user account. They are never transmitted to any party other than Google.

To disconnect Google Drive sync, click Disconnect in Settings. To fully revoke access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove KaptureVault.

No third-party sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your captured data to any third party. No analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking libraries are included in KaptureVault.

No accounts required

KaptureVault requires no registration, login, or account of any kind. The optional Google sign-in is used solely to access your own Google Drive storage.

Deleting your data

To delete all locally stored data, uninstall KaptureVault and then delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\KaptureVault\. To remove synced data from Google Drive, delete the KaptureVault folder from your Drive.

Children's privacy

KaptureVault is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be reflected in updated releases of KaptureVault and on this page. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
